What we do

Roofing for Southern Indiana, done once and done properly

Residential and commercial. Every job starts the same way — somebody gets on the roof and photographs what is actually there.

Roof replacement

When repair is not enough. Premium materials, a detailed estimate with several options, and a lifetime workmanship warranty.

Roof repair

Shingle and flashing repair, vent boots, ridge caps, soffit and fascia, valleys. We diagnose the actual problem, not the symptom.

Storm damage

Hail, wind and fallen trees. We document everything, meet your adjuster on site, and handle the claim from end to end.

Seamless gutters

Aluminium gutters measured and formed on site, plus downspouts and gutter guard systems that keep debris out.

Commercial & flat roofing

TPO, EPDM, modified bitumen, standing seam metal and built-up systems for businesses, warehouses and churches.

Emergency tarping

Tree through the roof or an opening letting weather in? We secure the area and install heavy-duty tarping that holds.

Storm & insurance

The claim is where most homeowners lose money

Most standard policies cover damage from hail, windstorms, fallen trees, fire and lightning. Getting paid for it is a different job to fixing the roof.

What we do for you

We work the claim with you

  • Free storm damage inspection with full photo documentation
  • We meet your adjuster on the roof and walk it with them
  • We advocate for the full scope of work, not a patch
  • Emergency tarping first if the roof is open to weather
Watch out for

How to spot a storm chaser

  • They knocked on your door unsolicited after the storm
  • They want full payment upfront
  • The bid is dramatically lower than everyone else
  • They are pushing you to sign today

A local contractor has a reputation to protect. They cannot do bad work and disappear.
